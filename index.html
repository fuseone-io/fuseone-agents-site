02 / Governance

Not a feature. The architecture.

01 — THE GATE

No effect bypasses it

Seven checks, four verdicts. Every tool call is allowed, blocked, or held for a person — by policy a Curator owns, never by the agent's author or the tool's server. There is no "just this once".

02 — THE LEDGER

Append-only, hash-chained

No UPDATE, no DELETE — the database itself refuses. Corrections are new steps, so the trail your auditor reads is the one that actually happened, sealed step by step.

03 — APPROVALS

Copilot before autonomous

An agent earns autonomy one approval at a time. The ask lands where your team lives, and a yes releases exactly one held effect, once — recorded with who said it.

03 / How it runs

From trigger to report, one sealed trail.

01

Trigger

Cron, webhook, or someone asking in a channel. Every run opens with an owner, a budget, and an idempotency key.

02

Engine

The loop folds the ledger to decide the next action. State is derived from the trail, never stored beside it.

03

Tools

MCP servers, classified centrally. A server may offer a tool; what it is allowed to do is the Curator's ruling.

04

Report

A one-line headline in the channel, the full report in its thread — with cost and the sealed trail a click away.
